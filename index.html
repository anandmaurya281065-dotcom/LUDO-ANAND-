Classic Ludo Rules

  • Unlocking Tokens: Roll a 6 to move a token from your home yard onto your colored starting square.
  • Bonus Rolls: Rolling a 6, capturing an opponent, or reaching the center home triangle grants an extra roll!
  • Triple-6 Rule: Rolling three 6s in a row forfeits your turn immediately.
  • Captures & Safe Stars: Landing on an opponent on a regular square sends their token back to their yard. Colored start squares and ★ star squares are safe zones where tokens coexist peacefully.
  • Winning: Bring all 4 of your tokens into the center home triangle by exact dice count!
